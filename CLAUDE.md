## The Product

*What it does, for whom, current focus.*

Streakly is a consumer habit + micro-learning app: users pick a track and do a short daily lesson to learn a skill in five minutes a day, building a streak. The streak is the core habit loop. Launched 4 years ago, Series B funded ($42M), 2.1M registered users, 340K MAU, growing 28% YoY on MAU.

Working scenario: the **Streakly Comeback experience** — recovering Day-7 retention, which slipped from 48% to 39% after the streak redesign shipped.

**Core metric:** Day-7 retention.

**Key tension:** the streak mechanic that drives the core habit loop is the same mechanic causing users to feel punished and go passive when it breaks.

**Current open decision:** whether the root cause is the streak reset mechanic itself, the "you lost your streak" notification tone/timing, or both — plus the specifics of the proposed Comeback screen (who's eligible, streak-freeze rules). Pending alignment at the team's Thursday meeting. See [01-orient/project.md](01-orient/project.md) for the working PRD.

**Standing constraint:** the Comeback concept is buildable with existing systems — no new data sources needed.
