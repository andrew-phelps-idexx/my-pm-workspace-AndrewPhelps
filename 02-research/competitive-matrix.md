## Duolingo

- **Core features:** gamified language lessons, streak mechanic, leaderboards/leagues, XP/gems currency, streak freeze (protects a streak for 1-2 missed days), streak wagers.
- **Pricing model:** freemium — free tier with ads, "Super Duolingo" subscription (ad-free, unlimited hearts, etc.), "Max" tier with AI features. Exact current price points were not verified in this pass.
- **Target customer:** broad consumer market, casual-to-committed language learners.
- **How they keep users engaged after week 1:** streak freezes are auto-granted as refills at milestone tiers (e.g., every 100-day streak grants 3 more freezes) so protection is already in place before a user needs it; streak wagers have been shown to boost Day-14 retention by 14%.
- **Notable recent changes:** in June 2026, ran a limited-time event letting users who had lost a streak of 30+ days restore it for free by completing 3 lessons in a row; also increased the price of the streak freeze on iOS.
