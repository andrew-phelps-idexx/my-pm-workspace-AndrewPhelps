# Interview Synthesis — Streakly User Interviews

*Source: 3 interview excerpts — Priya S. (power user, 14-month streak), Tom R. (churned user, broke a 12-day streak at 5 weeks), Amara L. (new user, 4 days in).*

*Note: with only 3 interviews, some themes below are strongly supported by only one speaker rather than two. Quotes are verbatim from the excerpts provided — no quotes were invented to fill out the format.*

## Top 5 Themes

### 1. Fear of losing streak progress (loss aversion)

- Amara L.: "I don't want to lose everything I've built after four days."
- Tom R.: "I got to a 12-day streak and I was proud of it."

### 2. All-or-nothing reset feels punishing

- Tom R.: "Then I traveled for work, missed two days, and came back to a big fat zero."
- Tom R.: "I switched to Duolingo, at least there a missed day doesn't wipe everything, and a streak freeze feels forgiving."

### 3. Celebration and positive reinforcement build commitment

- Priya S.: "The thing that made it stick was hitting a 30-day streak and the app actually celebrating it, that little moment of 'look what you built.'"
- Priya S.: "After that I did not want to lose it."

*(Only Priya spoke directly to this theme — no second independent voice.)*

### 4. Early habit formation is fragile and takes weeks

- Priya S.: "But honestly it took me about three weeks to get there, and I think most people quit long before the habit forms."
- Amara L.: "I check it, but the pressure is starting to feel like a chore instead of a game."

### 5. Notification tone adds negative pressure at the worst moment

- Tom R.: "The app sent me this 'you lost your streak' notification that just made me feel bad."
- Tom R.: "There was no way to recover it, nothing. So I gave up."

*(Only Tom spoke directly to this theme — no second independent voice.)*

## Contradictions / Tensions

- **The same mechanic retains and repels.** Priya's loyalty is built on the streak + celebration mechanic ("look what you built"), while Amara's day-4 anxiety comes from that same mechanic before she's ever had a chance to experience the reward. What retains power users appears to be what's stressing out new ones.
- **Streakly's model vs. the competitive bar.** Tom doesn't just criticize the reset in the abstract — he names Duolingo's streak-freeze as the standard he expected and didn't get. This is a competitive gap, not only an internal UX complaint.
- **Timeline mismatch.** Priya believes "most people quit long before the habit forms," estimating that at around three weeks. Tom's actual churn happened later — at 5 weeks, after a 12-day streak — suggesting even a partially-formed habit isn't safe from a single break, later than Priya's model would predict.

## Single Most Important Insight for Week-1 Retention

Amara is the only genuine week-1 voice in this set, and she hasn't broken a streak yet — she's anxious about the *possibility* of losing her progress before any break has happened. That anticipatory dread is already turning the product from "game" into "chore" within her first 4 days.

This suggests the week-1 fix can't only be a post-break comeback screen (which addresses Tom's later-stage churn, after a break has already occurred) — it needs to address the anxiety *before* a break ever happens, since new users haven't yet banked the reward experience (Priya's 30-day celebration moment) that would offset that fear.
