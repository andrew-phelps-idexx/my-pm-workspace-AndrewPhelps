# Strategy — Streakly Comeback Experience

*Where does this fit in the bigger picture?*

## Hypothesis

Winning users back the moment they break a streak recovers the 9-point Day-7 retention drop (48% → 39% since the streak redesign shipped).

## Why We Believe This

The key tension: the streak mechanic that drives the core habit loop is the same mechanic causing users to feel punished and go passive when it breaks. Today, breaking a streak is met with a cold reset and no acknowledgment — no comeback path is offered at the exact moment users are most likely to churn.

If the moment of the break is where users disengage, then intervening at that moment — rather than after the fact — is where retention can be recovered.

## How This Connects

The proposed Comeback screen (best-streak stat, 60-second comeback lesson, one-tap streak-freeze) is the current best guess at what "winning users back in the moment" looks like. See [project.md](project.md) for the full PRD and open questions, still pending alignment at Thursday's meeting.
