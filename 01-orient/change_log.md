# Change Log

*Running log of decisions and what changed, and why.*

| Date | Change | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| 2026-09-28 | Day 1 of discovery phase for the Streakly Comeback experience. | Kicking off discovery to validate the hypothesis that winning users back at the moment they break a streak recovers the 9-point Day-7 retention drop. |
| 2026-09-30 | Softened the streak-freeze offer screen (step 2) in the prototype: changed the headline from "Protect your next streak" to "We've got you next time," removed the bordered/highlighted "feature pitch" card styling, and changed CTAs from "Turn on streak freeze" / "Skip for now" to "Sounds good" / "Not now." | Persona-based usability testing (Priya, Tom, Amara) found the freeze offer read as a sales pitch to the skeptical churned-user persona (Tom) and re-triggered failure-anxiety in the anxious new-user persona (Amara) — the opposite of its intended effect. Both personas flagged this screen specifically; it was the single highest-priority issue since it undermines the core JTBD for the exact segment the screen exists for. |
| 2026-09-30 | Removed the forced ~4-second disabled-button delay on the comeback-lesson screen (step 3). "Mark lesson complete" is now clickable immediately; the progress bar remains as ambient visual flavor only, no longer gating the action. | A second round of persona-based usability testing found the softened freeze screen landed well, but Tom (the skeptical churned-user persona) flagged the artificial wait for the second time, calling it "busywork" with no functional purpose. It was the clearer of two friction points raised (vs. the still-large streak number on screen 1, a milder stylistic issue) since it's pure friction with zero benefit and directly affects the target persona's willingness to re-engage. |
| 2026-09-30 | Changed the flow so the streak freeze only activates upon completing the comeback lesson (added explanatory text to the freeze screen). Accepting the freeze now routes to the lesson (step 3); declining now skips the lesson entirely and jumps straight to the success screen (step 4), which now shows a different message depending on path taken. | Requested change to make the freeze's dependency on lesson completion explicit and to shorten the path for users who decline the freeze. Note: this changes the previously logged success condition ("user completes the lesson") — a user who declines the freeze can now reach the success screen without completing the lesson. |
